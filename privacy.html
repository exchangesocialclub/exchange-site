Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Exchange Social ("Exchange Social," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Exchange Social mobile application and this website (together, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. The Service is intended only for adults aged 18 or older.

1. Who we are

The Service is provided by Exchange Social App, LLC, a North Carolina limited liability company. For privacy questions or requests, contact us at privacy@exchangesocialapp.com.

2. Age requirement

You must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction, if higher) to use the Service. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected information from a person under 18, we will delete it. Where required by law, we use age-assurance and identity-verification measures to confirm that users are adults.

3. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information — email address, date of birth, and login credentials.
  • Profile information — the information you choose to add, such as display name, age, photos, videos, bio, interests, and relationship details.
  • Location information — approximate location used to power proximity-based discovery. You control location sharing through your device and app settings.
  • Age-verification data — where verification is required, this is handled by a third-party verification provider. We receive a pass/fail result and limited status information; we do not store your government ID.
  • Content — messages, photos, videos, and other content you upload or send through the Service.
  • Usage and device information — app activity, device identifiers, IP address, and diagnostic data.
  • Payment information — subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple and Google. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

4. How we use your information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the Service, including location-based discovery and matching.
  • To verify your age and identity where required.
  • To keep the Service safe — including content moderation, fraud prevention, and detecting prohibited content.
  • To communicate with you about your account, updates, and support.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

5. Content moderation and child-safety

We use automated and manual review to detect and remove prohibited content. We use tools designed to detect child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We have zero tolerance for CSAM. Where we identify apparent CSAM, we will remove it, preserve information as required by law, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or other authorities as required by applicable law.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • With other users — the profile information and content you choose to make visible.
  • With service providers — vendors who help us operate the Service (for example, hosting, age verification, content moderation, analytics, and email), under contractual confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal reasons — to comply with law, enforce our terms, protect safety, or respond to lawful requests.
  • In a business transfer — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact privacy@exchangesocialapp.com. You can also edit your profile, adjust location and privacy settings, and delete your account within the app.

8. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as needed to provide the Service and for legitimate business or legal purposes. Certain information related to safety and legal compliance may be retained longer where required by law.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International users

The Service is operated from the United States. If you access it from outside the U.S., your information may be transferred to and processed in the U.S. and other countries.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and revise the "Last updated" date. Material changes will be communicated as required by law.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email privacy@exchangesocialapp.com.
